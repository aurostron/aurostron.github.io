Who I Am

Making
stuff, since always?

I'm aurostron. A developer who got hooked on making things interactive, then never stopped. My work lives mostly in the browser — games, experiments, visual tools — stuff that gives people something to do, or at least something to have fun with.

It doesn't matter where you start. It matters how you progress from there.

I built hoGAMEGATA as a minimalist, fast database made with ❤️ for horror games. It catalogs over 18,000 titles. No ads, no paywalls, or tracking shit. Just a pure discovery tool for horror fans.

Outside of that, I am exploring stuff. Different tools, different ideas, different aesthetics. The internet is huge and weird and full of things worth trying. My projects are one of them :)
