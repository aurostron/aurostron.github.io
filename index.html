Who I Am

Making
stuff, since always?

I'm aurostron. I build tools and archives for the web. Lately that means projects like hoGAMEGATA, a 107,000-game horror database, and OpenLyryc, an in-browser lyric and karaoke timer. I make things that run fast, work right in the tab, and don't require an account or show ads.

It doesn't matter where you start. It matters how you progress from there.

Most of my projects start from personal curiosity or simple frustration with existing software — clunky interfaces, heavy sign-up walls, or lost media that never got cataloged. The goal is always the same: keep things lightweight, private, and open for anyone to use.

Outside of those, I'm usually experimenting with new web APIs, tinkering with creative code, and seeing what else can run cleanly inside a browser tab.
